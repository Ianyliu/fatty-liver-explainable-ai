\section*{Data and code availability}
The ultrasound data originate from Taiwan Biobank and acquisition at MacKay Memorial Hospital \citep{yen2024fattyliver}. Patient images, metadata and trained weights are subject to access restrictions. Analysis and reproducibility scripts are available at \url{https://github.com/Ianyliu/fatty-liver-explainable-ai}; use requires authorized access to the restricted inputs.
